Privacy

In short: this page sets no cookies and builds no profiles. It only counts how often it is viewed, without recognising individual visitors.

Hosting

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub B.V. (Prins Bernhardplein 200, 1097 JB Amsterdam, Netherlands) and GitHub, Inc. (San Francisco, USA). When you visit the page, GitHub says it logs and stores your IP address for security purposes. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in running the page securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for this. More in GitHub's privacy statement.

Visitor count

To see how often the page is read, it uses GoatCounter. The service is run by Martin Tournoij (Ireland); the data is stored on Hetzner servers in Finland and Germany.

It records the page viewed, the page you came from, browser and operating system, screen width, language, and the country derived from the IP address. GoatCounter does not store IP addresses, sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. To recognise repeat views, the site name, IP address and browser identifier are compared in memory for at most 8 hours and are not stored permanently. Only aggregated counts per hour and day are stored. The data is not shared with third parties.

The legal basis is the legitimate interest in knowing how often the page is read and where it is shared (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More in GoatCounter's privacy policy.

Stored in your browser

If you change the language or dark mode, the page remembers your choice in your browser's storage (localStorage). It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere. It is needed for the setting you chose to work (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Font and links

The font is served from the same server as the page; there is no connection to Google Fonts or similar services. The source links lead to other websites. Only when you click one do you connect to that website, and its privacy policy applies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of your data, and to object to the processing (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
